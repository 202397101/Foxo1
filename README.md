# Tubular Cell Senescence Disrupts FOXO1-Dependent Renal Gluconeogenesis During Starvation in Chronic Kidney Disease

---

**Abstract**  
Renal gluconeogenesis contributes to systemic glucose production during starvation, but its regulation and disruption in chronic kidney disease (CKD) remain poorly defined. Here, we investigated FOXO1-dependent renal gluconeogenesis during starvation and its impairment in CKD. Integrated bulk and single-cell RNA sequencing revealed that starvation induces a proximal tubule–centered gluconeogenic program associated with FOXO1 activation. Renal tubule–specific Foxo1 deletion suppressed gluconeogenic enzyme expression and exacerbated fasting-induced hypoglycemia, establishing renal FOXO1 as a critical regulator of glucose homeostasis under starvation. In CKD models, basal glucose levels were preserved despite reduced FOXO1 activity and suppressed gluconeogenic gene expression; however, prolonged starvation caused profound hypoglycemia with impaired FOXO1 activation and blunted renal gluconeogenesis. Public human CKD single-cell transcriptomic analysis revealed an inverse association between proximal tubular senescence and gluconeogenic gene programs. Consistently, senescent tubular cells showed reduced FOXO1 activity, impaired gluconeogenic gene expression, and diminished glucose production under starvation. Similar defects were observed in aged kidneys and validated in human CKD kidney samples. These findings identify tubular senescence as a key mechanism disrupting FOXO1-dependent renal gluconeogenesis and adaptive glucose homeostasis during starvation in CKD and aging.

**Overview of repository**  
*Summary of R Script Execution*  
There are seven R scripts, each corresponding to a specific figure.  
To run these scripts, you must first download the necessary data sets.
The data can be obtained from either a provided compressed file (Figure_Rdata.zip) or from the databases detailed below.
After downloading, appropriate data processing is required before executing each script.

1. **For Figure 1**  
Starvation induces proximal tubule–centered metabolic reprogramming in the kidney. 

2. **For Figure 3**  
Starvation activates FOXO1 to drive renal gluconeogenesis. 

3. **For Figure 5**  
Starvation-induced renal gluconeogenesis is impaired in fibrotic kidneys.

4. **For Figure 6**  
Tubular senescence is associated with suppression of renal gluconeogenesis in human CKD and experimental senescence models. 

5. **For Figure 9**  
Human CKD kidneys exhibit tubular senescence-associated suppression of FOXO1-dependent renal gluconeogenesis.
